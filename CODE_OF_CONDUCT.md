# Community Code of Conduct

This project exists to make authorized cybersecurity learning more practical and accessible. Participation requires professional, lawful, and respectful behavior.

## Expected behavior

- Share reproducible, technically accurate information.
- Test only systems you own or are explicitly authorized to assess.
- Sanitize logs, screenshots, credentials, tokens, and client data.
- Give constructive review feedback and credit upstream work.
- Clearly label legacy, destructive, unreliable, or detection-sensitive techniques.
- Prefer official documentation and maintained projects as sources.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Requests or contributions targeting real systems without authorization.
- Doxxing, credential sharing, malware distribution, harassment, or discrimination.
- Fabricated results, copied work without attribution, or intentionally unsafe commands presented as routine steps.
- Publishing private reports or exploit details that violate a coordinated disclosure process.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and the project. Report sensitive concerns privately through the process in `SECURITY.md`.
